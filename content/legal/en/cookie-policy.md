## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser when you visit it. They let the site remember certain information, such as your preferences, or measure how it is used.

Besides cookies, we also use similar technologies such as browser local storage (localStorage) and tracking pixels. For simplicity, this policy calls all of them "cookies".

Cookies can be:

- **first-party**, set by our own website;
- **third-party**, set by other providers whose services we use, such as Google, Meta or TikTok;
- **session cookies**, deleted when you close your browser;
- **persistent cookies**, kept for a set period.

## 2. How we ask for consent

**Analytics and marketing cookies do not load until you give consent.** If you reject them, the site keeps working normally. Only strictly necessary cookies are used without consent, because the site cannot work properly without them.

## 5. How to change or withdraw consent

You can change your choices at any time:

- switch the categories you want on or off;
- save your preferences.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Once you withdraw, optional cookies are no longer loaded. Cookies already stored in your browser can be deleted through your browser settings, as described below.

## 6. Browser controls

Most browsers let you view, block or delete cookies. The options are usually in the settings menu, under privacy or security:

- **Google Chrome**: Settings, then Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies or Site data.
- **Mozilla Firefox**: Settings, then Privacy & Security.
- **Safari**: Preferences or Settings, then Privacy.
- **Microsoft Edge**: Settings, then Cookies and site permissions.

Blocking all cookies, including strictly necessary ones, may affect parts of the site. For example, your cookie preferences will not be remembered and the banner will appear on every visit.

You can also limit personalised advertising directly in your Google, Meta or TikTok account settings.
